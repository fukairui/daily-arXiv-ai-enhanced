### [1] [When and How to Ask: Dynamic Preference Elicitation Strategies for Conversational Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06765)
*Feng Xia,Shuo Zhang,Xi Wang*

Affiliations: Bloomberg, University of Sheffield

Main category: cs.IR

AB Test: False | Industrial: True | Affiliation: collaboration

TL;DR: 本文系统研究了对话推荐系统中偏好引导策略的阶段感知特性，发现最优策略依赖对话阶段：早期属性询问有效，后期基于物品的策略更优。提出了带细粒度标注的InPE数据集和基于混合专家模型的COPE架构，离线实验证明上下文感知的偏好引导策略有益于对话推荐。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有对话推荐系统多采用静态的偏好引导策略，且主要关注显式属性询问，忽略了基于物品的偏好引导以及策略在不同对话阶段的变化。本文旨在探索最优策略的阶段依赖性和上下文敏感性。

Method: 系统实证研究、InPE数据集（含细粒度偏好引导必要性和策略选择标注）、COPE架构（基于混合专家模型的策略建模）。

Result: 离线实验表明上下文感知的偏好引导策略优于静态策略；策略分析揭示了对话推进中的阶段性交互模式。

Conclusion: 最优偏好引导策略是阶段依赖和上下文敏感的：早期属性询问有效，后期基于物品的策略更优。上下文感知的策略建模能提升对话推荐性能。

Abstract: Conversational Recommender Systems (CRSs) are interactive systems that use multi-turn natural language dialogue to understand evolving user preferences and provide personalized recommendations. To achieve this goal, CRSs rely on preference elicitation strategies to actively gather informative preference cues from users; however, the timing and selection of these strategies during a conversation remain largely unexplored. While many existing studies emphasize eliciting explicit item attributes and tend to adopt relatively static elicitation strategies, the use of item-based preference elicitation and how it varies across different dialogue stages remains less explored. In this work, we conduct a systematic investigation of preference elicitation strategies from a stage-aware perspective. We provide empirical evidence that optimal preference elicitation strategies are stage-dependent and context-sensitive: attribute-based inquiries are effective in early stages, while item-based strategies become superior as preferences refine. To support this paradigm, we introduce InPE, a dataset enriched with fine-grained annotations for elicitation necessity and strategy selection. With this dataset, we propose COPE (COnversational Preference Elicitation via Mixture of Experts), a novel architecture for strategy modeling. Extensive offline evaluation on our dataset indicates that context-aware preference elicitation strategies are beneficial for conversational recommendation. In addition, the analysis of the predicted strategies uncovers consistent stage-wise tendencies in dialogue progression, providing empirical evidence of common interaction patterns in conversational recommendation systems. Our dataset is available at https://github.com/juanfacabian/InPE.

### [2] [Seeing and Reflecting: Multimodal Memory-Enhanced Agent Collaboration for Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07108)
*Hao Cong,Huizu Lin,Zihan Wang,Chengkai Huang,Quan Z. Sheng,Lina Yao*

Affiliations: CSIRO, Tsinghua University, USTC, PKU

Main category: cs.IR

AB Test: False | Industrial: True | Affiliation: collaboration

TL;DR: MMEACR提出了一种多模态记忆增强的智能体协作推荐框架，通过双轨记忆架构（推理轨+匹配轨）分别建模可解释的智能体推理和细粒度多模态匹配，并采用加权倒数排名融合来整合两个轨道的结果，在三个真实场景中取得了优于现有LLM和智能体基线的效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于LLM的智能体推荐系统受限于文本中心输入和粗粒度记忆更新，容易丢失视觉证据、引入语义噪声并难以应对偏好漂移。

Method: 提出双轨记忆架构：(1) 推理轨中，User和Item记忆智能体通过属性引导的反思-强化机制维护持久多模态记忆；(2) 匹配轨中，从原始交互叙述和物品图像构建解耦的多模态嵌入记忆以保留细粒度跨模态信号；两轨道通过加权倒数排名融合(RRF)整合。

Result: 在三个真实世界领域（包含视觉导向的推荐场景）上，MMEACR在总体性能上优于竞争性的LLM基线和智能体基线，在视觉导向推荐场景中提升尤为显著。

Conclusion: 多模态记忆增强的智能体协作能有效弥补纯文本推理的不足，通过分离推理与匹配两个轨道实现了既鲁棒又可解释的推荐。

Abstract: Large language model (LLM)-based agentic recommender systems show promise in modeling user preferences through natural-language reasoning, yet they remain limited by text-centric inputs and coarse-grained memory updates, making agents prone to missing visual evidence, semantic noise, and preference drift. To address these limitations, we propose MMEACR, a Multimodal Memory-Enhanced Agent Collaboration framework for recommendation. MMEACR introduces a dual-track memory architecture that separates interpretable agent reasoning from fine-grained multimodal matching. In the reasoning track, collaborative User and Item Memory Agents maintain persistent multimodal memories and update them through an attribute-guided reinforcement-and-reflection mechanism. In the matching track, a decoupled multi-modal embedding memory is built from raw interaction narratives and item images to preserve detailed cross-modal signals beyond structured memory updates. The two tracks are integrated through weighted Reciprocal Rank Fusion to produce robust and interpretable rankings. Experiments on three real-world domains show that MMEACR achieves strong overall performance against competitive LLM-based and agent-based baselines, with notable gains in visually grounded recommendation scenarios.

### [4] [Interpretable Uncertainty for Adaptive Retrieval and Reasoning in Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07380)
*Ritajit Dey,Iadh Ounis,Graham McDonald*

Affiliations: University of Glasgow

Main category: cs.IR

AB Test: False | Industrial: False | Affiliation: academia

TL;DR: 提出了一种基于LLM内部表示中显式不确定性信号的适应性问答框架，通过区分知识不足与知识歧义/冲突，在单次前向传播中高效估计这些信号，从而决定何时触发检索（RAG）或额外推理，为QA系统提供透明且高效的适应性策略。

Motivation: LLM在问答中易产生幻觉且缺乏透明度；现有RAG方法在决定何时检索时依赖不透明策略或低效的多步提示，缺乏可解释的适应性机制。

Method: 从LLM隐藏状态中分解估计两种不确定性信号：知识不足（触发RAG检索）和知识歧义/冲突（触发额外推理），所有信号在单次前向传播中高效计算。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) achieve a strong performance in question answering (QA), but remain prone to hallucinations and suffer from limited transparency. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) can improve factuality, yet decisions about when and how to retrieve from external resources are typically based on opaque policies or computationally inefficient multi-step prompting procedures. We propose an uncertainty-aware framework for adaptive QA based on explicit signals derived from LLM internal representations. We distinguish between knowledge insufficiency and knowledge ambiguity or conflict, and efficiently estimate these from hidden states in a single forward pass. These signals guide system behaviour: RAG is triggered when knowledge is insufficient, while additional reasoning is applied when ambiguity or conflict is high. By grounding adaptive decisions in decomposed and efficiently estimable uncertainty signals, this approach provides a transparent and practical alternative to existing retrieval and reasoning strategies supporting the design of interpretable user-facing tools.

### [5] [InductWave: Inductive Multi-Hop Logical Query Answering on Knowledge Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07422)
*Mayank Kharbanda,Michael Cochez,Rajiv Ratn Shah,Raghava Mutharaju*

Affiliations: IIIT-Delhi, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam

Main category: cs.AI

AB Test: False | Industrial: False | Affiliation: academia

TL;DR: InductWave是一种基于小波的归纳式嵌入方法，用于知识图谱上的逻辑多跳查询回答，在训练图节点更少的情况下，能以更少的消息传递层数达到或超越现有基线模型性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有逻辑查询方法主要是归纳式的，无法推理训练中未见过的实体；现实世界资源稀缺，无法用KG所有节点训练模型。

Method: 提出InductWave，一种基于小波变换的归纳式嵌入方法，用于大规模KG上的逻辑查询回答。训练图包含比测试图更少的节点，模型使用小波变换进行消息传递。

Result: 模型在基线模型相当的情况下只需一半的消息传递层数；在75%的层数设置下超越所有基线模型。在FB15k-(237)数据集上进行了广泛的实验验证。

Conclusion: InductWave在资源需求更低的情况下达到或超越现有方法，能够处理大规模KG如Wiki-KG。

Abstract: Logical Multi-Hop Query Answering over Knowledge Graphs (KGs) can be formulated as querying, with an implicit completeness assumption. Current works mainly focus on Existential First Order Logic (EFO) queries. These EFO queries contain conjunction, disjunction, and negation operators. Most existing works employ transductive reasoning, meaning they are not capable of reasoning over entities unseen during training. In the real world, there is a resource scarcity, and we cannot train a model with all the nodes of a large KG. Hence, we propose InductWave, a wavelet-based inductive embedding method for logical query answering on large KGs. Here, the training graph consists of fewer nodes than the test graph. Our model performs on par with the baseline models while having half the number of message-passing layers. It outperforms all of them in most cases, with 75% of the layers. These fewer resource requirements enable us to evaluate InductWave on massive graphs, such as Wiki-KG. We test our model using extensive experiments across varying train-test graph proportions of the FB15k-(237) dataset, comparing it with the state-of-the-art models. The code and datasets for the model are available at https://github.com/kracr/inductwave/.

</details>
