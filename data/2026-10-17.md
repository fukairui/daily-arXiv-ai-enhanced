### [1] [ReviewPilot: A Multi-Agent System for Screening and Data Extraction in Systematic Reviews](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21042)
*Zhaoqian Xue,Shaoze Zhou,Xuexin Li,Junting Mu,Haozheng Liu,Zhongyin Wang,Yichun He,Renkai Ma,Hao Zhou,Sarah Bratt,Siyuan Ma,Chen Chen,Lingyao Li*

Affiliations: University of Arizona, Vanderbilt University, FIU

Main category: cs.CL

AB Test: False | Industrial: False | Affiliation: academia

TL;DR: ReviewPilot 是一个对话式多代理系统，自动化 PRISMA 流程中的文献筛选与信息抽取，并通过项目记忆、决策理由和来源溯源提升可追溯性，在流行病学专家评审中优于单提示 LLM 和 AgentSLR。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基于 LLM 的深度研究工具可检索和总结文献，但不记录被排除的研究及抽取数值的来源，无法满足 PRISMA 系统综述的可复现与可审计要求。

Method: 构建对话式多代理系统 ReviewPilot：研究者通过对话定制筛选标准与抽取模式；项目记忆保存配置与修订；每个筛选决定附理由，每个抽取值附来源。

Result: 在流行病学专家评审数据上，于三种 LLM 上均优于单提示基线和 AgentSLR，筛选 macro F1 达 0.776，抽取 F1 达 0.788；生物医学与人机交互案例与人工标注高度一致。

Conclusion: ReviewPilot 能端到端自动化 PRISMA 工作流并具备可溯源性，且可迁移至流行病学之外领域。

Abstract: Systematic literature reviews follow the PRISMA process of screening scholarly works and extracting evidence from included studies. Deep research tools based on large language models (LLMs) can search and summarize the literature but do not record which studies are excluded or where extracted values come from. We develop a conversational multi-agent system called ReviewPilot that automates the PRISMA workflow from paper screening to information extraction. Researchers can tailor criteria and schemas through dialogue, and a project memory keeps their configurations and revisions. Each screening decision comes with its reason and each extracted value with its source. On epidemiological expert reviews, ReviewPilot outperforms a single-prompt LLM and AgentSLR across three LLMs, with screening macro F1 up to 0.776 and extraction F1 up to 0.788. Case studies in biomedicine and human-computer interaction show close agreement with human annotators, suggesting that ReviewPilot can transfer beyond epidemiology. Code is available at https://github.com/ZhaoqianXue/ReviewPilot.

### [2] [Learning When to Think: Utility-Guided Adaptive Reasoning for Efficient Text Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21030)
*Yingtao Luo,Kangyi Zhao,Shiyi Du,Yubo Li*

Affiliations: CMU, University of Pittsburgh

Main category: cs.IR

AB Test: False | Industrial: False | Affiliation: academia

TL;DR: AURA：用中间推理状态对相关文档与难负例的区分度作为效用信号直接监督推理过程，并训练轻量效用估计器在推理时自适应决定推理深度；BRIGHT上AURA-4B以2.8步、96个token达到34.7 nDCG@10，延迟由4.6s降至0.76s。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 推理条件化嵌入（reasoning-conditioned embeddings）能逐步细化语义约束以提升检索，但过量推理会引入过度泛化或语义噪声的抽象，反而削弱检索特异性。现有方法多以最终检索结果作为监督信号，对推理细化过程本身缺乏指导，也无法按需决定推理深度。

Method: 提出AURA框架：(1) 依据每个中间推理状态对相关文档与难负例（hard distractors）的区分能力，直接监督中间推理状态，而非仅用最终检索结果；(2) 在前缀条件化嵌入（prefix-conditioned embeddings）上训练轻量级效用估计器（utility estimator），在推理时自适应选择推理深度/提早停止。

Result: 在推理密集型BRIGHT基准上，AURA-4B平均nDCG@10为34.7，每查询平均仅用2.8个推理步、生成96个token。匹配部署条件下，自适应停止将延迟从4.6秒降至0.76秒（相对完整轨迹推理）。MS MARCO结果进一步表明该专门化不会显著损害常规域内检索性能。

Conclusion: 以中间推理状态的检索效用作为过程监督信号，并结合轻量效用估计器做自适应停止，可在保持检索精度的同时显著降低推理步数与延迟，且对常规域内检索的负面影响有限。

Abstract: Reasoning-conditioned embeddings can improve retrieval by progressively refining semantic constraints. However, additional reasoning may introduce over-generalized or semantically noisy abstractions that weaken retrieval specificity. Existing methods largely supervise reasoning through final retrieval outcomes, providing limited guidance over the refinement process itself. We propose AURA, a framework that directly supervises intermediate reasoning states according to how well they separate relevant documents from hard distractors. AURA further trains a lightweight utility estimator over prefix-conditioned embeddings to adapt reasoning depth at inference time. On the reasoning-intensive BRIGHT benchmark, AURA-4B achieves 34.7 average nDCG@10 while using 2.8 reasoning steps and 96 generated tokens per query. Under matched deployment conditions, adaptive stopping reduces latency from 4.6 to 0.76 seconds relative to full-trajectory reasoning. Results on MS MARCO further suggest that this specialization need not substantially degrade conventional in-domain retrieval.

### [3] [RecSoup: Efficient Model Merging Approach for Controllable Multi-Objective Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21220)
*Zidong Jia,Guofu Xie,Xiao Zhang*

Affiliations: Renmin University of China

Main category: cs.IR

AB Test: False | Industrial: False | Affiliation: academia

TL;DR: RecSoup通过两阶段模型融合（跨目标感知剪枝+可学习偏好到系数的自适应合并）实现可控多目标推荐，推理延迟比参数生成方法快165–228倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 可控多目标推荐需在推理时动态调节目标权衡而无需重训；现有方法依赖输出级重排（可行权衡范围受限）或参数生成模块（训练与推理开销大）。模型融合虽可复用多目标模型，但推荐场景缺乏共享线性区且存在跨目标参数干扰，直接应用困难。

Method: 两阶段轻量方法：第一阶段采用同时考虑目标特定重要性与跨目标相关性的剪枝策略，缓解目标间参数干扰；第二阶段通过可学习的偏好到融合系数映射，进行自适应参数合并，实现推理时动态权衡控制，无需联合重训或额外生成模块。理论上证明其在无平滑性约束下可完整遍历Pareto前沿，优于参数生成范式。

Result: 多个基准实验表明Pareto前沿质量与可控性更好，推理延迟较参数生成方法降低165–228倍。

Conclusion: 轻量两阶段模型融合可有效支撑可控多目标推荐，兼顾前沿质量、可控性与效率。

Abstract: Recommender systems often need to balance multiple conflicting objectives. Controllable Multi-Objective Recommendation (CMOR) enables dynamic adjustment of objective trade-offs at inference time without retraining, adapting to shifting user or system requirements. Existing CMOR approaches typically rely on output-level reranking or parameter-generating modules, which either limit the scope of feasible trade-offs or incur substantial training and inference cost. Model merging provides a promising alternative by reusing multiple objective-specific models via parameter combination. However, its direct application to recommender systems is challenging due to the lack of a shared linear regime and cross-objective interference. To address these issues, we propose RecSoup, a lightweight two-stage approach for effective CMOR via specialized model merging. RecSoup first mitigates parameter interference across objectives through a pruning strategy that jointly considers objective-specific importance and cross-objective relevance. It then performs adaptive parameter merging via a learnable preference-to-coefficient mapping, allowing dynamic trade-off control at inference time without joint retraining or additional generative modules. Theoretically, we prove that RecSoup outperforms the parameter generation paradigm in effectiveness and efficiency, as it can fully navigate the Pareto front without smoothness constraints. Extensive experiments on multiple benchmarks demonstrate that RecSoup achieves improved Pareto front quality and effective controllability while delivers 165--228x faster inference-time latency than parameter generation approaches. Code is available at https://github.com/35798642/RECSOUP.git.

</details>
