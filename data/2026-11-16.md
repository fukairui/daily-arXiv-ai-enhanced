### [1] [StreamEmbed: Real-Time Indexing from Pixels for Screenshot Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18516)
*Dayoon Ko,Yichuan Wang,Soyeong Jeong,Joseph E. Gonzalez,Gunhee Kim,Sewon Min*

Affiliations: UC Berkeley, Seoul National University

Main category: cs.CV

AB Test: False | Industrial: False | Affiliation: academia

TL;DR: StreamEmbed：利用截图像素冗余的增量视觉嵌入系统，通过空间token剪枝与时间KV复用，在保持精度的同时将屏幕截图编码加速至3.7倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 屏幕历史可作为可搜索的视觉记忆，但需随每张截图实时编码索引。OCR索引虽快却丢失视觉信息，视觉编码耗时超两秒。屏幕内容增量变化，大部分像素在截图内和截图中重复，因此视觉编码器无需对每张新截图从头重算表示。

Method: 仅由像素差异驱动的增量视觉嵌入：空间token剪枝（跳过与邻居重复的块）+ 时间KV复用（复用跨截图未变化块的缓存键值），并训练小型适配器补偿剪枝与复用带来的偏差，以保持与全图嵌入的一致性。

Conclusion: 统一的增量编码与轻量索引使原生视觉形式的屏幕历史实时索引成为可能，为完全本地的视觉助手奠定基础。

Abstract: Screen history can serve as searchable visual memory for people and agents, but unlike an offline corpus, it must be encoded and indexed as each screenshot arrives. OCR-based indexing is fast but loses visual information; visual encoding takes over two seconds. Yet screens change incrementally: most pixels repeat within and across screenshots. This raises a question: do visual encoders need to recompute their representations from scratch for every new screenshot? We present StreamEmbed, a system for incremental visual embedding that exploits this redundancy. Guided solely by pixel differences, it combines spatial token pruning, which skips blocks that repeat a neighbor, with temporal KV reuse, which reuses cached keys and values for blocks unchanged across screenshots. A small learned adapter compensates for pruning and reuse to preserve fidelity to full-screenshot embeddings. On ScreenMemEval, our benchmark of 10,426 questions over 184 hours of screen histories from two humans and a computer-use agent, StreamEmbed sets a new accuracy-latency Pareto frontier on an M2 Pro GPU: it encodes up to 3.7x faster than full-screenshot encoding without sacrificing accuracy and improves accuracy over OCR-based indexing by up to 6.3 Recall@10 points at matched latency. A memory-efficient vector index stores a simulated year of screenshots in 0.13 GB of RAM. Together, these results make real-time indexing of screen history in its native visual form practical, laying a foundation for fully local visual assistants.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出RecMem：将历史切分为片段，用共享底层、分支式编码器-解码器把片段压缩进固定数量的记忆槽；槽级遗忘门控做递归更新以实现终身演化；以推荐监督信号训练记忆保留预测信息；引入流一致性训练，使顺序处理与打包（packed）并行处理的预测对齐，从构造上保证记忆状态与解码上下文长度与历史长度无关，并在收缩性与近似半群一致性假设下推导出两种处理顺序间状态差异的与视界无关的上界。

Conclusion: 固定容量记忆槽+短解码上下文的循环压缩可在显著降低序列处理成本的同时近似保留长历史建模能力，且远历史信息并非无用，验证了记忆压缩路线的可行性与可演化性。

Abstract: Generative recommendation benefits from long interaction histories, but the cost of processing them directly grows with history length. We propose RecMem, a recurrent memory compression framework guided by three desiderata: space invariance, semantic fidelity, and lifelong evolvability. RecMem compresses historical segments into a fixed set of memory slots using a bifurcated encoder-decoder with shared lower layers and slot-wise forget-gated updates. Recommendation supervision trains the memory to preserve predictive information, while stream-consistency training aligns predictions from sequential and packed processing. By construction, the memory state and decoder context are independent of history length; under contraction and approximate semigroup consistency, we further derive a horizon-independent bound on the state discrepancy between these two processing orders. On MerRec, RecMem uses 16 memory slots and a 216-token decoder context, compared with 1,200 tokens for HSTU-1200, while retaining 87.9% of HSTU-1200's Recall@10 and 97.0% of its Recall@200. It outperforms PHSTU and the evaluated recent-only and retrieval baselines, and analyses of distant history show that archived interactions contribute predictive signal.
